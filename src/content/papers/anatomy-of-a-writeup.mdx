## Explorable figures

A language model turns logits $z_i$ into a distribution with a temperature-scaled
softmax:<Sidenote>At $T \to 0$ this becomes argmax; as $T \to \infty$ it approaches
uniform over the vocabulary.</Sidenote>

$$
p_i = \frac{\exp(z_i / T)}{\sum_j \exp(z_j / T)}
$$

The equation is easier to understand once you can change $T$ yourself.

## Linked views

When two views show the same data, hovering in one should light up the other.
Here the arc diagram and the heatmap both show one head's attention weights.

## Scroll-driven scenes

For a narrative over one big visual, pin the graphic and let the text scroll past it.
Each step below sets a shared value that the canvas listens to.

<Scrolly id="cloud">
  <EmbeddingCloud slot="graphic" scrollyId="cloud" client:visible />
  <Step>
    **Noise.** Four thousand points, drawn from an isotropic Gaussian. Drag to rotate.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Clusters.** Each point moves to its group's center. Their colors are still hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Labels.** Coloring by group shows that the clusters were real.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Structure.** The groups lie along a single one-dimensional curve, ordered.
  </Step>
</Scrolly>
